The assessment model

Quantified risk is the product of how likely a threat is and how much it could cost. The model splits that into two branches and resolves each with dedicated assessments, then runs the parameters through tens of thousands of Monte Carlo rounds to produce a financial range with a confidence interval.

Select any node in the diagram to dig in.

Tip: the three black nodes are the engines. Everything light feeds or results from them.
Parameters are expressed as three-point tuples (min · most likely · max) following a Beta-PERT distribution, giving the simulator a realistic range rather than a single guess. Likelihood and impact are then simulated jointly to generate the quantified result. This diagram is a generic, vendor-neutral rendering of a scenario-based CRQ methodology.
