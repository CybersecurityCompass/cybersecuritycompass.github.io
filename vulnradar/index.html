This tool is based on the article Vulnerability Prioritization in the Age of the Vulnpocalypse: Avoiding the Anything2Shell Hype. It is a helper to organize the manual work of prioritization, not a platform that calculates cyber risk for you. Start with your own exposure, then understand the vulnerability, then decide. Six dimensions in the order of the Cyber Risk Management Lifecycle (CRML), each with a scope for your organization and for your third parties. The CRML is a continuous cycle, not a one time review, and this assessment is only one turn of it. Answer with evidence, not opinion. Every “Unknown” is treated as exposed, and the dashed line shows how much of your result depends on what you have not confirmed yet.
